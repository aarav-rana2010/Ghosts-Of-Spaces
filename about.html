A LITTLE ABOUT ME

HELLO, I'M AARAV RANA.

I am a student with a growing interest in aerospace engineering, space science and technological innovation. What fascinates me most about aerospace is the combination of imagination, engineering and scientific understanding required to explore beyond our planet. From spacecraft design and planetary exploration to the science behind robotic missions, I enjoy discovering how humanity continues to push the boundaries of what is possible. GHOSTS OF SPACE is one way I have chosen to turn that curiosity into something tangible: a project that combines my interest in space with research, storytelling and web development.

01 / WHERE IT BEGAN

A FASCINATION WITH AEROSPACE.

My interest in aerospace engineering comes from a fascination with the technology that allows humanity to explore places far beyond Earth. Space exploration has always represented something extraordinary to me. It brings together physics, mathematics, engineering, creativity and the determination to understand the unknown.

The more I explored the subject, the more I became interested in the machines themselves: the rovers, landers and spacecraft that travel millions of kilometres to investigate other worlds. Each machine represents years of planning, engineering and scientific ambition.

That interest eventually became the foundation of GHOSTS OF SPACE.

02 / WHY GHOSTS OF SPACE?

THEIR MISSIONS ENDED. THEIR STORIES DIDN'T.

Space exploration is often remembered through its biggest moments: rocket launches, historic landings and extraordinary discoveries. But there is another side to these missions that deserves attention.

What happens to a spacecraft after its mission ends? What remains of the machines that once explored another world? What discoveries did they make, and how did they change our understanding of the universe?

Some explorers continue to rest on the lunar surface. Others remain on Mars, silent reminders of missions that transformed planetary science. Their journeys may be over, but the scientific knowledge they helped create continues to influence future exploration.

I wanted to create a platform that brings these stories back into focus. A place where visitors can explore forgotten explorers and understand why their journeys mattered.

03 / THE SCIENCE BEHIND THE STORIES

MORE THAN MACHINES. A LEGACY OF DISCOVERY.

GHOSTS OF SPACE is not intended to be just a collection of spacecraft photographs or mission descriptions. Every explorer has a scientific story.

The Lunar Roving Vehicle expanded the range of human exploration on the Moon. Surveyor 3 became part of an important chapter in understanding the lunar environment. Sojourner demonstrated the possibilities of mobile robotic exploration on Mars. Spirit and Opportunity helped uncover evidence of ancient water and the planet's geological history.

These missions demonstrate how even machines that have stopped operating can continue to contribute to science. Their discoveries form part of the foundation upon which future missions will be built.

05 / BUILDING GHOSTS OF SPACE

FROM AN IDEA TO AN INTERACTIVE EXPERIENCE.

Building GHOSTS OF SPACE has been an opportunity to combine different interests into one project. I wanted the website to do more than display information. I wanted visitors to be able to explore, interact and discover things at their own pace.

The project brings together research into historic space missions, individual mission archives, planetary exploration and scientific storytelling. I also worked on the website's visual identity, navigation, interactive planetary map, animations and responsive layouts.

Developing it has been a learning experience in itself. Each feature has involved understanding how to translate an idea into a functional digital experience using web technologies.

06 / THE VISION AHEAD

KEEPING THE SPIRIT OF EXPLORATION ALIVE.

I see GHOSTS OF SPACE as a project that can continue to grow. There are countless missions, spacecraft and discoveries waiting to be explored. Each one has a unique story and a contribution to humanity's understanding of space.

My vision is to make this platform an engaging place for anyone curious about space exploration, whether they are students, enthusiasts or simply people who enjoy discovering the history of science and technology.

More importantly, I hope this project reflects the curiosity that first inspired it: the desire to understand what lies beyond the familiar.
